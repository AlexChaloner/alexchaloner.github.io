Flow Matching Diagrams

Diffusion predicts noise, which the sampler uses to work out how to move. Flow matching learns that motion directly. Let’s build the intuition with handwritten digits.

Zero to Five

The same six zeros start both routes. Diffusion was trained on fives and treats the starting zero as noise; flow matching was trained on matched zero-to-five pairs. This is a comparison of those two setups.
