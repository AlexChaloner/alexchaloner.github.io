A Grounded Interactive Explanation of Flow Matching

Flow Matching [paper link here] has become the go-to algorithm for Diffusion models. If you're like me, you will be familiar with Noise Diffusion models which simply apply noise to an input gradually, then train to denoise. When trying to learn flow matching, I have seen this 'field diagram' which I feel is hiding why we care about flow matching. Here, we base everything directly onto MNIST.

FIRST, THINK ABOUT THE LATENT SPACE, I.E. THE LOW-DIMENSIONAL REPRESENTATION OF YOUR INPUT. HERE, WE THINK ABOUT MNIST COMPRESSED INTO 2 DIMENSIONS BY TAKING THE PCA OF A GOOD MODEL. (These PCs remain unchanged for the entire blog post). Very simply: Diffusion was modelling what needed to change from step i to step i+1. Flow Matching is modelling directly a vector field of all changes.
